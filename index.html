Support · Privacy policy

Kitbox is a box of model kits for building tiny dioramas on your iPhone or iPad. Pick a kit and a plot, then snap pieces onto the grid: a street of shops, a snowy village with a toy train, a pirate cove with a dock, a candlelit dungeon.

What's inside

Roads, rails, walls, fences and paths join up by themselves as you drag. Buildings turn to face the road beside them. Undo and redo always work, and two fingers turn and zoom the view. When you like what you've made, light it for day, golden hour, blue hour, night, dawn or mist, take a photo, and share it or save it to your photo library.

Seven kits

City and Village are free. Space Base, Dungeon, Pirate Cove, Spooky Hollow and Snow Day are one purchase each, yours to keep, or get Every Kit: all five, and every kit added later at no extra cost.

No ads. No tracking.

No ads of any kind, no timers, no coins and no account. Kitbox collects nothing and works without a connection. It speaks English, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese.

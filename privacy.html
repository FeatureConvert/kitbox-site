Kitbox Privacy Policy

Kitbox: Tiny Diorama Builder, by Nexrax · Last updated October 5, 2026 (saving and sharing photos)

Short version: Kitbox doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game makes no network connections of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. Kitbox has no accounts, no ad networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no servers of its own. We don't know who plays, and we never see your data.

Your plots, photos and settings

Everything you make stays on your device: your plots, the photos you take in photo mode and your settings (sound, music, language and so on). They're kept in the app's own storage. A photo only leaves it when you tap Share on it: your device's own share sheet opens, and you choose where it goes (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, another app) or Save Image, which puts it in your photo library. Kitbox asks your device for permission to add to your photo library the first time you save one, and never reads your photo library. If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, this data goes along with that backup, as it does for any app; we can't see it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game only learns which kit you bought so it can open it for you, and Restore Purchases asks Apple for the same thing.

Children

Kitbox is made for everyone, including families. Because we collect no personal information, we don't collect any from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

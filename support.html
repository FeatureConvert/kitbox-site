Common questions

I bought a kit but don't have it.

Tap Kits on the home screen, then Restore at the top right. (It's also in Settings, as Restore Purchases.) Every kit you bought comes back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. A kit bought with Ask to Buy arrives once it's approved.

Does Every Kit include kits that come out later?

Yes. Every Kit opens all the kits for sale now, and every kit added later at no extra cost.

How do I move my plots to a new iPhone?

Your plots and photos are kept on your device, so they move with it when you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup. Kitbox has no account or cloud copy of its own. Kits you bought come back on the new iPhone with Restore.

How do I rename or delete a plot?

On the home screen, tap the ⋯ on the plot's card, then Rename or Delete plot. Deleting asks first, and can't be undone.

Can I change the language?

Kitbox follows your iPhone's language. To pick another one, open Settings and tap Language.

Are there ads?

No. Never. No timers or coins either.
